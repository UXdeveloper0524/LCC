Back to School Package

A single visit that gets your child ready for the new term, with results explained the same day.

What’s included

One visit, one clear picture.

Term time is easier when you know your child is well. This package brings the essentials together in one appointment with our pediatric team, so there’s no need to come back for separate checks.

  • Pediatric consultation and general examination
  • Vision screening
  • Hearing screening
  • Height, weight and growth review
  • Vaccination record review
  • Doctor’s summary and advice for the school year

How the visit works

  1. 01

    Arrive

    Reception checks your insurance and settles you in.

  2. 02

    Screening

    Vision, hearing and growth checks with our nursing team.

  3. 03

    Consultation

    Your pediatrician reviews everything and answers your questions.
