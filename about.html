About us

Care built around people.

Life Care Clinic is a multi-specialty polyclinic where expert medicine and genuine warmth sit side by side.

Our story

A clinic designed around the patient, not the paperwork.

We opened Life Care Clinic with one idea: healthcare should feel calm. Everything here is arranged around that — unhurried consultations, a connected team of specialists under one roof, and clear explanations you can act on.

Our doctors work across family medicine, pediatrics, women’s health, internal medicine, dentistry and wellness, so most of what you need happens in one place, with one team who knows your history.
